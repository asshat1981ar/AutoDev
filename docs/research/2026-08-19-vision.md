# End vision

AutoDev is an Android-first, local-first, multiplatform software-development system where a user can describe an objective and supervise a durable team of specialized agents through planning, implementation, verification, recovery, and delivery.

The system interoperates with modern harness/plugin/skill/MCP ecosystems without surrendering its trusted execution kernel. Long runs survive process/network interruption. Every consequential effect is capability-scoped and observable. Completion is independently evidenced. Android provides a clear, user-friendly control/review surface while heavier work can execute locally or through trusted companions according to environment capability.

AutoDev improves its routing, toolsets, profiles, and workflows through controlled evidence-backed evaluation rather than unchecked self-modification.
