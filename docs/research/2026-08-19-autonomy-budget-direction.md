# Autonomy-budget direction

Autonomy should be expressed through explicit budgets rather than a binary autonomous/manual mode. Candidate budgets include maximum replans, attempts per milestone, concurrent workers, context/model spend, elapsed time, and risk/capability ceilings.

Budget exhaustion transitions to a diagnosable blocked/failed state with preserved evidence. Higher autonomy changes how long the system may continue within policy; it does not change what effects it is authorized to perform.
