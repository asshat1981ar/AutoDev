# Development research policy

Use research to resolve concrete architectural/API uncertainty, not as a substitute for implementation/evidence. Prefer primary specifications, source repositories, release notes, and papers with code/results when evaluating mechanisms.

Record only material findings in repository artifacts and state what design decision they influence. Refresh fast-moving sources at milestone start. Competing approaches should be evaluated against AutoDev's invariants and, where practical, tested through prototypes/benchmarks rather than selected by popularity.
