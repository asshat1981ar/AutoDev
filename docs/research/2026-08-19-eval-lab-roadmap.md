# Simulation/Eval Lab roadmap

1. Reuse the current self-evaluation factory's frozen historical tasks and protected surfaces.
2. Add durable-run traces and fault-injection scenarios.
3. Represent experimental configuration tuples explicitly.
4. Add metrics for recovery, unsafe-action rejection, context/resource efficiency, and human intervention.
5. Support controlled ablations and repeated/seeded trials.
6. Add held-out promotion evaluation.
7. Feed evidence-backed recommendations to routing/toolset/profile selection without granting automatic policy mutation.

The lab should test AutoDev itself as both product and development harness while preserving independent verification.
