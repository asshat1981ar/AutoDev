# Review defect priorities

Critical: authority bypass, unsafe replay, fabricated verification/completion, data-loss/destructive behavior.

Important: invalid lifecycle/recovery state, unbounded retries, serialization corruption, compile/API defect, CI contract not actually enforced.

Minor: naming/documentation ergonomics, non-load-bearing refactors, research organization.

Critical/Important findings block Milestone 1 exit until fixed or explicitly adjudicated after the defined review loop.
