# Authority taxonomy

- **Intent:** what a user/agent/harness requests.
- **Declared capability:** what an asset/action says it needs.
- **Policy eligibility:** whether trusted policy permits the requested class/scope.
- **Approval:** trusted scoped human/system authorization when required.
- **Execution authority:** kernel-owned ability to perform the exact validated effect.

Portable manifests, model outputs, plan state, and plugin trust labels live in the first two categories. They never skip directly to execution authority.
