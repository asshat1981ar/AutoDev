# PR risk assessment

Production-code risk is moderate and localized to a new ForgeCore domain module plus export. It does not alter existing execution/policy paths. Primary risks are compile/format/API defects, insufficient lifecycle validation, and future callers misinterpreting reconciliation acknowledgement as trusted evidence.

Mitigations: focused adversarial tests, explicit ADR/docs, dedicated CI, and later replacement of boolean reconciliation with typed evidence during durable orchestration.
