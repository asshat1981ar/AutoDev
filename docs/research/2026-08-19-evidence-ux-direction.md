# Evidence UX direction

Verification should be presented as a first-class product object, not a terminal log dump.

For each required check, show name, status, provenance/executor, timestamp, relevant artifact/log reference, and why the check is required. Distinguish `not run`, `running`, `passed`, `failed`, and `unknown/unsupported` states. Overall completion must visibly explain when otherwise-passing work is blocked by missing required evidence.
