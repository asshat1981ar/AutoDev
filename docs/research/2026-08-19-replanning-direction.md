# Replanning direction

Replanning changes the future route, not the historical record. A replan should preserve completed/verified milestones, decisions, discoveries, evidence, and consumed budgets; explain why the previous plan became inadequate; and produce a new bounded set of future tasks.

Repeated deterministic failure should converge toward blocked/failed diagnosis rather than prompt churn. Replan budgets can later become task/risk dependent, but they remain finite and policy-visible.
